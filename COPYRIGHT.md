## Source of the Materials

The following materials in this repository are extracted from YouTube:

- **Video metadata** (`info/`): video URLs, titles, descriptions, and chapter listings, taken from the public video pages of the channel.
- **Subtitles** (`transcript/`): subtitles downloaded from YouTube with `yt-dlp` and minified into `processed/`.
- **Persian summaries** (`content/`): blog posts derived from those subtitles and written by this project; they are original Persian-language texts, but they are derivative works of the source videos.
